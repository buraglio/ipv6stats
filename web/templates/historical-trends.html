{% call reference(reviewed="2026-10-14") %}

  • 8 June 2011: World IPv6 Day.
  • 6 June 2012: World IPv6 Launch.

  • July 2017: RFC 8200 replaces RFC 2460 as the IPv6 standard.

  • 19 November 2020: OMB memorandum M-21-07 sets US federal IPv6-only targets.
